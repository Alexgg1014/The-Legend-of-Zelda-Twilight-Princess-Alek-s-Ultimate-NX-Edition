Not under a license: the ideas taken from it are used with the author's permission (2026-10-19). They are
the CPU-cached staging slots (COS_DK_CACHED_UPLOAD) and the present's colour clear left out when the

sa2-nx and banjo-nx (ChanseyIsTheBest), peggle2-nx, futari-nx, nfsmw-nx
Ideas only: no file, code or text of these projects is included (sa2-nx is GPL-3.0); the code here is our own.
From Chansey's sa2-nx and banjo-nx: showing a late frame at the next refresh instead of two later
(vsync_relaxed, off by default), returning early from the hook dispatch when there is nothing to do,
writing GPU timestamps only while something reads them, the profiler's stack-bound check, and the
renderer A/B switches in perf-lab.ini (zcull, colour compression; off by default), and the
profile-guided build workflow (profile run on the console, then -fprofile-use). From peggle2-nx:
syncing the shader cache to the card as it grows. From futari-nx: writing the log from a background
thread instead of flushing every line. From nfsmw-nx: asking the system for its official handheld
configuration with the GPU at 460.8 MHz -- the "GPU Boost (handheld)" setting, off by default
(src/dusk/gpu_boost.cpp). Each place says so in its comments. Thanks to Chansey and to these projects.
